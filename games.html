Games

Four games on Steam, newest first. Three are out; one arrives in Q1 2027. Two of them are free.

RoboSquare: Recharged

Coming Q1 2027

PC, Steam · $5.99 USD · Not Early Access

An online multiplayer party game about hovering robots and the appalling things they do to each other. Sixteen players, five randomly chosen minigames back to back, and one champion at the end. The empty seats fill themselves, so a full sixteen-bot brawl is always one click away.

Steam page · Press kit

Glingdom

Out now

PC, Steam · $5.99 USD · Released 7 November 2024

A medieval roguelike deckbuilder. Beat waves of enemies and bosses, buy cards and runes to strengthen your deck between fights, and unlock new units as you go. Over fifty of them.

Steam page · Press kit

RoboSquare

Out now, free

PC, Steam · Free to play · Released 31 December 2021

The first RoboSquare. Free to play, up to sixteen robots, and three modes that all came down to the same thing: push the other robot off the platform. Nearly 54,000 people played it. It is still up and still free.

Steam page · Press kit

Sandbox Anything

Out now, free, Early Access

PC, Steam · Free · Early Access since 18 December 2019

Fizzostia's first Steam release. A physics building game where you throw objects around to build structures that solve the level. It ships with a level editor, Steam Workshop support and online multiplayer.

Steam page · Press kit

A note on the free ones

RoboSquare and Sandbox Anything are both still up and both still free. RoboSquare is where the audience for Recharged came from, and Sandbox Anything is where all of this started. Neither is being actively developed any more; the work goes into Recharged.
