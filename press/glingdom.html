Glingdom

A medieval roguelike deckbuilder. Beat waves of enemies and bosses, buy cards and runes to strengthen your deck between fights, and unlock new units as you go. Over fifty of them.

Key features

  • Waves and bosses. Fight through enemy waves, then something bigger at the end of them.
  • Buy between fights. Cards and runes, spent on making the next run survivable.
  • 50+ units. Unlocked by playing, mixed into decks that fight on their own once placed.
