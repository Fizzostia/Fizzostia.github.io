RoboSquare: Recharged

An online multiplayer party game about hovering robots and the appalling things they do to each other. Sixteen players, five randomly chosen minigames back to back, and one champion at the end. The empty seats fill themselves, so a full sixteen-bot brawl is always one click away.

Key features

  • Sixteen seats, so nobody sits out. Most party games cap at four or eight and somebody in the group ends up watching. Bring six friends, or twelve, or fifteen.
  • Three verbs, endless mess. Jump, Dash, Slam. Momentum is your best weapon and your worst habit.
  • Eight chaos events fire mid-round, unannounced. Wind picks a direction and commits. Gravity gets lazy. Bombs fall. A UFO helps itself to whoever is winning.
  • The lobby is never empty. Any seat nobody is in gets filled, so the game still works at 3 am, or a year after launch.
  • Deep bot customization. 13 bodies, 6 hover thrusters, 6 finishes, plus body and thruster colors.
  • Accolades and rivalries. Every hit, jump, dash, slam and meter traveled is tracked, and the game works out who your rival is from how often you two keep crashing into each other.
