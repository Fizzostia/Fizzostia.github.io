RoboSquare: Recharged

An online multiplayer party game about hovering robots and the appalling things they do to each other. Sixteen players, five randomly chosen minigames back to back, and every round pays into your bot's vault. The biggest vault after five rounds wins.

The five modes

  • Delivery Race. Every checkpoint is a drop-off and every drop-off pays. Take them in any order you like. The golden crate at the finish only opens once the rest are delivered.
  • Vault Raid. Knock a bot off the platform and a cut of everything it has earned leaves with you. The platform is smaller than you think and your friends are worse than you remember.
  • Heist. A bank truck is driving around the arena with the round's money in it. Stay close and it pays out, and the fewer bots sharing, the bigger the cut. It does not stop for anyone.
  • Hold The Bag. A few bots are holding bags that pay every second. Touch one and the bag is yours, along with everyone who was chasing it.
  • Capture the Core. Team warfare over cores, where one dedicated slammer can ruin an entire strategy.

Key features

  • Sixteen seats, so nobody sits out. Most party games cap at four or eight and somebody in the group ends up watching. Bring six friends, or twelve, or fifteen.
  • Every round pays. All five modes pay into your bot's vault, and the vault carries through the match. Get knocked off in Vault Raid and part of it leaves with someone else.
  • Three verbs, endless mess. Jump, Dash, Slam. Momentum is your best weapon and your worst habit.
  • Eight chaos events fire mid-round, unannounced. Wind picks a direction and commits. Gravity gets lazy. Bombs fall. A UFO helps itself to whoever is winning.
  • Deep bot customization. 14 bodies, 6 hover thrusters, 6 finishes, plus body and thruster colors.
  • Accolades and rivalries. Every hit, jump, dash, slam and meter traveled is tracked, and the game works out who your rival is from how often you two keep crashing into each other.
