RoboSquare

The first RoboSquare. Free to play, up to sixteen robots, and three modes that all came down to the same thing: push the other robot off the platform. Nearly 54,000 people played it. It is still up and still free.

Key features

  • Three modes. Sumo, Boss of the Hill and Race, all built on shoving other robots off the arena.
  • Sixteen players. Online PvP with synchronized hazard events tearing up the arena mid-match.
  • Free, and staying free. Four cosmetic DLC bundles exist; nothing in them affects a match.
