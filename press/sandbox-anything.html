Sandbox Anything

Fizzostia's first Steam release. A physics building game where you throw objects around to build structures that solve the level. It ships with a level editor, Steam Workshop support and online multiplayer.

Key features

  • Throw things until the level is solved. Physics-enabled objects, stacked into structures that hold, or do not.
  • Built-in level editor and Steam Workshop. Make levels, publish them, play other people's.
  • Online multiplayer. PvP and co-op.
  • A crossover topper. Owning it unlocks a customization topper in RoboSquare.
