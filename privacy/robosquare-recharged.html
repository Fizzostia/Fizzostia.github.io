Privacy policy · updated 8 November 2026

RoboSquare: Recharged

The game records how you play (how long, where, which matches and maps, and when you leave) and sends it to me so I can make the game better. It's tied to your Steam account. I don't sell it, I don't show it to advertisers, and you can turn it off in Settings.

This policy covers the RoboSquare: Recharged game on Steam (app 4088870) and the online services it talks to. It's made and run by one person, Griffin Foster (Fizz), the game's solo developer. Where this policy says "I" or "me", that's who it means.

The demo (app 5525910) sends none of the gameplay stats described below.

What the game collects

While you play, the game sends a short report to my server every few minutes and whenever a match ends. If the game closes before a report gets out, it's saved on your PC and sent the next time you play.

Who you areYour SteamID and your current Steam name. Steam confirms the SteamID, so nobody can send stats as you.
Your countryA two-letter country code worked out from your connection by my host, Cloudflare. Not your city, not your address.
Each time you playWhen you started, how long you played, how long the game window was in the background, whether the game closed normally or crashed, whether it was your first launch, and the game version.
Where your time goesTime spent in each part of the game: each main menu screen (customization, crates, crew, leaderboard and so on), searching for a match, lobbies, rounds (by mode and map), between rounds, results, freeplay, Crew HQ, the tutorial and the map editor.
Your matchesWhen each match started and how long it lasted, whether it was private, your party size, how many players and bots were in it, how many rounds you played, and how it ended for you: finished, went back to the menu, or closed the game, plus the point and map you were on when you left.
How you didYour placement and money in each match and round, plus your match stats: hits, eliminations, times eliminated, jumps, dashes, slams, distance driven, time in the air and cash earned.
One-off momentsWhen a match search finds a game, gets cancelled or fails (and how long it took), and when you start, finish or leave the tutorial.
Your PCOperating system, processor and core count, memory, graphics card and its memory, screen resolution and refresh rate, window mode, graphics quality preset, system language, whether you use keyboard or gamepad, and your average frame rate during rounds.

What it doesn't collect

  • Your IP address. My host sees it to deliver the report, like any website does, but I don't store it.
  • Your messages or voice. Text and voice chat go between players through the game's networking service and are never recorded or sent to me.
  • Payment details. Purchases happen on Steam. I never see your card or your Steam wallet.
  • Anything outside the game. No files, browsing, other apps, contacts or precise location.
  • Your real name or email, unless you choose to write to me.

Why I collect it

I use these stats to fix and improve the game, and for nothing else:

  • spot maps and modes people leave early, and figure out why;
  • see how long matchmaking takes and when people give up on it;
  • find crashes and the hardware that struggles to run the game;
  • learn which parts of the menu people use, and which they skip;
  • understand how many people play, when, and whether they come back.

I look at the stats myself on a private dashboard. I don't sell them, rent them, or share them with advertisers or data brokers, and I don't use them to target ads at you.

For players in the EU, UK and similar places: the legal basis is my legitimate interest in running and improving the game (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by turning the stats off (see Your choices).

Where it's kept and who handles it

The stats are stored in a database run by Cloudflare, in the United States. If you play from somewhere else, your stats travel to the US. These companies help run the game:

Access to the stats is password-protected and limited to me.

How long I keep it

I keep gameplay stats while I'm working on the game and using them to improve it. If I stop running the game's servers, I delete them, and you can have yours deleted at any time (see Your choices). A report saved on your PC because it couldn't be sent is deleted from your PC once it's sent, or right away if you turn the stats off.

Your choices

Turn it off. Settings > Gameplay > Privacy > Share Gameplay Stats. With it off, the game sends nothing and deletes any unsent report from your PC. The game plays exactly the same either way.

Delete what I have. Email me at fizzostia@gmail.com and include your SteamID or a link to your Steam profile, and I'll delete all of your stats within 30 days. To make sure it's really you, I may ask you to confirm through Steam.

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to its use. Email me and I'll respond as your local law requires. If you're unhappy with how I've handled a request, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

I don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines those terms.

Other features that store data

A few features keep what they need to work, whether or not gameplay stats are on. They're stored with Cloudflare like the stats, and kept while the feature is in use.

  • Crews. Your SteamID and Steam name, the crew you're in and your role, invites sent and received, maps added to the crew, and a log of crew changes. Other members of your crew can see your name and role. Leaving a crew removes you from it.
  • Code redemption. Your SteamID, the code you entered, the item it gave you and when, so a code can't be claimed twice.
  • Feedback reports. Only when you send one from the menu: your message, its category, the game version, your Steam name and SteamID, your PC details and the game's log file from that session. The log can include folder paths on your PC, which may contain your Windows user name. Reports are stored in a Google Form only I can open.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13, and Steam requires players to be 13 or older. I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). If you think a child's data has reached me, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If I change what the game collects, I'll update this policy and its date before the change ships. Big changes will also be announced on the game's Steam page.
